Our Story

Three Decades of
Honest Real Estate

SOI Estates was born in Ludhiana in 1992 with a simple but powerful belief — that every person deserves to find the right property without confusion, pressure, or hidden agendas.

For over 30 years, we have operated as a fully independent firm. We are not tied to any builder, developer, or real estate corporation. We work for you — the client — and we work with you — businesses and corporations — when they need real property solutions.

Our core strength is our people, our local knowledge, and our unbreakable commitment to dealing only in properties that are completed, legally clear, and ready to be sold by the actual owner.

✅ Completed Properties Only
We never deal in under-construction or unauthorised properties — ever.
🏠 Owner-Listed Only
Every property we represent comes directly from the owner, not from any builder's inventory.
📜 Legal Clarity Guaranteed
Clear title, registry verified, no encumbrances — before we present it to you.
🤝 Truly Independent
We serve companies and corporates, but we never sell under any company's banner.
