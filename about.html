Who We Are

Independent. Honest.
Always On Your Side.

SOI Estates was established in 1992 in Ludhiana, Punjab, with one clear purpose — to be the real estate firm that people can truly trust. Not the one with the most builder tie-ups. Not the one pushing the most projects. The one that actually works for the client.

We are not a franchise. We are not a startup. We are not a portal. We are an experienced, independent real estate firm that has operated in this market for over three decades — through every boom, every slowdown, and every change the sector has seen.

Our independence is our greatest strength. We have never signed an exclusivity agreement with a developer. We have never taken a builder's incentive. We earn by serving you well — and only that.

What Makes Us Different

What We Will Never Do

🚫 Never push under-construction properties
We only deal in fully completed, ready-to-possess, ready-to-register properties. Period.
🚫 Never list unauthorised or disputed properties
Every property undergoes a full legal check before it reaches our clients — no exceptions.
🚫 Never sell builder or company inventory
We help companies find property, but we never become an agent of their projects. We work for buyers, always.
🚫 Never hide the real price
We tell you the true market value and negotiate your best deal — even if it means less brokerage for us.
Our Journey

Three Decades of Building Trust

1992

Founded in Ludhiana, Punjab

SOI Estates opens its doors in Ludhiana with a simple mission — to offer honest, transparent real estate services to families and businesses in Punjab. Our first office is set up near Hambran Road, the same area we still proudly serve today.

Late 1990s

Building a Reputation for Honesty

Through word of mouth alone, SOI Estates becomes known as the agency that never pushes clients into bad deals. Our refusal to work with builders or list unauthorised properties begins setting us apart in a crowded market.

2000s

Expanding Across Property Types

Responding to client demand, SOI Estates expands from residential into commercial, industrial, agricultural and investment properties — while maintaining the same strict standards of legal verification and client-first service for every category.

2010s

Going Pan India

Our network grows beyond Punjab. Clients across India — including NRIs — begin seeking our expertise for property transactions in multiple cities and states. SOI Estates becomes a Pan India firm while keeping its Ludhiana roots and values intact.

2020s

30+ Years Strong — and Growing

Over a thousand families served. Zero builder tie-ups. Still family-run. Still independent. SOI Estates enters its fourth decade with the same commitment that started it — finding you the right property, every single time.
