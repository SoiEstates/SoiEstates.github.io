Who We Are

Independent. Honest.
Always On Your Side.

SOI Estates was established in 1992 in Ludhiana, Punjab, with one clear purpose — to be the real estate firm that people can truly trust. Not the one with the most builder tie-ups. Not the one pushing the most projects. The one that actually works for the client.

We are a family-run firm — founded by Parveen Soi and now carried forward by his sons Sameer Soi and Rohan Soi. Three generations of the same family, the same values, and the same commitment to honest real estate.

We have never signed an exclusivity agreement with a developer. We have never taken a builder's incentive. We earn by serving you well — and only that.

What We Will Never Do

Our Commitments

🚫 Never push under-construction properties
Only fully completed, ready-to-possess properties. Period.
🚫 Never list unauthorised properties
Full legal check before any property reaches our clients.
🚫 Never sell builder or company inventory
We help companies find property but never sell their projects to buyers.
🚫 Never hide the real price
We tell you the true market value and negotiate your best deal.

Our Journey

Three Decades of Building Trust

1992

Founded in Ludhiana by Parveen Soi

SOI Estates opens its doors on Hambran Road, Ludhiana with a simple mission — honest, transparent real estate for families and businesses in Punjab.

1990s

Building a Reputation for Honesty

Through word of mouth alone, SOI Estates becomes known as the agency that never pushes bad deals. The firm's refusal to work with builders starts setting it apart.

2000s

Expanding Across Property Types

SOI Estates expands into commercial, industrial, agricultural and investment properties while maintaining the same strict legal verification standards.

2010s

Second Generation Joins — Pan India Growth

Sameer Soi and Rohan Soi join the family business, bringing fresh energy and modern expertise. SOI Estates expands its reach across Pan India.

2020s

30+ Years Strong — Three Together

Parveen, Sameer and Rohan Soi continue building the legacy together. Over 1000 families served. Zero builder tie-ups. The most trusted name in Ludhiana real estate.
